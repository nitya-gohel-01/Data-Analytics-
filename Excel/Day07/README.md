# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Excel**. The project focuses on analyzing a real-world E-commerce sales dataset containing **5,000+ sales records** and transforming raw data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, analysis, KPIs, Pivot Tables, and interactive visualizations.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance and helps identify important trends related to revenue, products, regions, payment methods, and customer behavior.

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

The dashboard provides insights into:

### 💰 Total Revenue
Provides an overall view of the revenue generated from E-commerce sales.

### 📦 Total Quantity
Displays the total quantity of products sold across all transactions.

### 📊 Revenue by Payment Method
Helps analyze customer purchasing preferences based on different payment methods.

### 🛍️ Total Revenue by Category
Shows which product categories generate the highest revenue.

### 🌍 Total Revenue by Region
Helps identify the regions contributing the most to overall sales.

### ⭐ Revenue by Customer Rating
Analyzes the relationship between customer ratings and generated revenue.

### 📅 Year-wise Revenue Trends
Tracks revenue performance over time and helps identify sales trends.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project was completed through the following steps:

1. **Data Understanding**  
   Reviewed the E-commerce sales dataset and identified important columns for analysis.

2. **Data Cleaning**  
   Cleaned and prepared the dataset for analysis in Microsoft Excel.

3. **Data Analysis**  
   Used Excel features to analyze sales performance and identify meaningful patterns.

4. **Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts**  
   Created Pivot Tables and visualizations to summarize and explore the data.

5. **KPI Creation**  
   Developed key performance indicators such as Total Revenue and Total Quantity.

6. **Dashboard Development**  
   Combined KPIs, charts, and visualizations into an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.
